I am a last year **Ph.D student** at [Inria Paris](https://www.inria.fr/centre/paris), using neural networks for historical low-resourced situations. 

My research focuses on the evolution of words through time, such as: 
- designing neural networks for historical word reconstruction
- probing models to understand which historical information is latently learned
- studying these methods' transferability to NMT low-resource situations
- using language models for historical data.
