I am a last year **Ph.D student** at [Inria Paris](https://www.inria.fr/centre/paris), working on using neural networks for historical low-resourced situations. 

If it's about outreach, ping me!

## My research
So far, it focuses on the evolution of words through time: 
- designing and implementing the best possible architectures for historical word reconstruction (cognates, proto-forms) using methods inspired by low-resource machine translation
- interpreting/probing models to understand which historical information is learned as a by-product of our tasks
- studying the transferability of these methods to lexicon induction in low-resource situations. 

I also dabbled a bit in language models for historical data -- semantic evolution of words, or NER for historical texts. 

Before, I was a **software/research engineer** from 2015 to 2019.
