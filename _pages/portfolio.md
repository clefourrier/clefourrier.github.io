## 1) Database
### [EtymDB2](https://github.com/clefourrier/EtymDB) - Etymological database: 
<img src="/images/logos/etymdb2_logo.png" alt="The logo of EtymDB2: a tree sprouting of a database" width="90px" style="float: left; margin: 0 15px 0 0;"> Fine grained etymological database which differentiates between inheritance, cognacy, borrowing, and different types of composition.  
It comes with several Jupyter notebooks for data analysis, including one to draw your own language trees from the word links. 

Details in our [paper](http://www.lrec-conf.org/proceedings/lrec2020/pdf/2020.lrec-1.392.pdf): *Methodological Aspects of Developing and Managing an Etymological Lexical Resource: Introducing EtymDB 2.0.* 


## 2) Software
### [PLexGen](https://github.com/clefourrier/PLexGen) - Conlang generator:
If you want to try conlanging, you can with PLexGen, my phonetic lexicon generator and sound change applier!  
Provide your language shape (phonetics and phonotactics) and sound changes, and it will randomly generate a proto-language and its daughter languages after your specifications! 

### [CopperMT](https://github.com/clefourrier/CopperMT) - Cognate Prediction Per Machine Translation:
Scripts and algorithms to run cognate prediction tasks using machine translation (MOSES for SMT, or my multiway encoder decoder based on fairseq for NMT).

Details in our [paper](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-acl.75.pdf): *Can Cognate Prediction Be Modelled as a Low-Resource Machine Translation Task?*
