## Games I really recommend
- Hollow Knight (gorgeous design, beautiful story building, very hard but rewarding)
- TIS-100 (learn assembly programming!)
- Factorio (build your factory to leave the planet you are stranded on - very nice to watch what other players do, some develop beautiful design patterns - easy to lose a lot of time playing)
