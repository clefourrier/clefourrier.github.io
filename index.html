MEDeA (Multiway Encoder Decoder Architecture) for multilingual-translation architecture experiments

Inria - SED - ALMAnaCH

2019, Jan – Present Paris


I designed its architecture and coded the whole thing.

  • Model: Encoder-Decoder, with siamese networks (VAE ongoing work)
  • Options: Attention (Luong, Bahdanau), Teacher forcing, Batch and layer dropout, Loss combination (Seq2seq + siamese)
  • Reproducibility: It is fully parametrisable with json files, and every experiment is recorded with a console, tensorboard and file logger.
