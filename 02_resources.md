# NLP Resources {#resources}
### Database
#### EtymDB2 (an etymological database, [here](https://github.com/clefourrier/EtymDB)): 
Fine grained etymological database which differentiates between inheritance, cognacy, borrowing, and different types of composition.  
It is provided with several Jupyter notebooks for data analysis, including one to draw your own language trees from the word links. (Perl, Jupyter notebooks)  
More detail in our LREC [paper](http://www.lrec-conf.org/proceedings/lrec2020/pdf/2020.lrec-1.392.pdf): *Methodological Aspects of Developing and Managing an Etymological Lexical Resource: Introducing EtymDB 2.0.* 


### Software
#### PLexGen (a conlang generator, [here](https://github.com/clefourrier/PLexGen))
If you want to try conlanging, you can with PLexGen, my phonetic lexicon generator and sound change applier!  
Provide your language shape (phonetics and phonotactics) and sound changes, and it will randomly generate  
a proto-language and its daughter languages after your specifications! (Python)
